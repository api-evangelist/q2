Our September analysis of the Q2 PrecisionLender commercial loan and deposit pricing database looks at August 2026 activity, when pricing volume pulled back sharply after several months of increases. The decline showed up across segments rather than in one corner of the market. Underneath that pullback, funding and liquidity costs kept climbing, and fixed-rate and SOFR net interest margin moved into near parity after diverging for most of the year.
