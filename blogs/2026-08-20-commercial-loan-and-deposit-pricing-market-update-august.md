Our August analysis of the Q2 PrecisionLender commercial loan and deposit pricing database looks at July 2026 activity, and for the first time in several months, the picture is broadly positive. Spreads expanded across all three rate structures, coupons rose in tandem, and net interest margin improved on both fixed and floating deals.
