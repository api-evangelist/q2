Q2 Assistant is the unified AI intelligence layer embedded across Q2's product portfolio, giving financial institution teams a single interface to ask questions, search documentation, and complete tasks in natural language without leaving their workflow. Built to inform, guide, and act inside real banking operations, Q2 Assistant connects to product-specific agents that deliver task-level capabilities within each Q2 solution, governed by the security and compliance controls financial institutions require.
