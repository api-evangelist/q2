An informal Q2 analysis of about 20 customer banks—including eight of the largest—shows that adoption rates for commercial information reporting sit at roughly 10% to 15%. That’s a strikingly low percentage for accessing the critical automated clearing house (ACH), wire, and balance reports commercial clients need to run their businesses.
