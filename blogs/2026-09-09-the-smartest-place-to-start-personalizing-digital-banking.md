Most personalization advice suggests financial institutions start with one audience and build out. Q2's guidance is different: Choose the high-value groups at the edges, where needs differ most. The audiences at the ends of the user journey are where differentiation is most valuable, most visible, and, perhaps surprisingly, most achievable.
