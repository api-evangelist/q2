As Q2 Innovation Studio celebrates five years, PayRecs shows what that milestone looks like in practice: embedded payments that help financial institutions (FIs) support business customers wherever those customers operate. One of Innovation Studio's earliest partners, PayRecs built its international payments capability on Q2's APIs, marketplace, and prebuilt integrations, making it easier for FIs to bring that capability to market. At CONNECT 26, PayRecs walked through how its platform brings global payments, incoming payments, and multicurrency accounts into digital banking.
