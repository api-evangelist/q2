In any financial institution that’s working toward more streamlined, more meaningful, and more strategic processes and metrics, once an operating rhythm is in place, there’s a temptation to believe the hardest work is behind you. Reporting is integrated, pricing is tighter, portfolio review is more consistent. From a distance, the system looks sound.
